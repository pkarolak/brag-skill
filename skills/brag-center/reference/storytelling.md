# Storytelling

## Shape of the portfolio

1. **Hero**: one sentence positioning ("Builds the internal tools 3,000 people use every day"), name, title, team. Specific, not adjectives.
2. **Impact strip**: 3-5 numbers or crisp facts (apps shipped, users reached, time saved, launches, teams unblocked).
3. **Case studies**: 3-6 projects, strongest first (angle-relevant first).
4. **Voices**: 2-6 testimonials from Slack/Glean, attributed with name + role.
5. **Timeline**: dated milestones across the window.
6. **Toolbox**: skills/stack inferred from evidence (not a logo wall).

## Case study structure

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| `title` | Outcome-first: "Cut onboarding setup from days to minutes", not "Onboarding tool" |
| `context` | 1-2 sentences: the world before. Who hurt and how. |
| `challenge` | The hard part. Constraints, ambiguity, scale. |
| `role` | What *they* did. First person in `narrative.md`, third person on site unless the subject is the runner. |
| `moves` | 3-5 concrete decisions/actions, each one line, verbs first. |
| `outcome` | What shipped and what changed. |
| `impact` | Metrics + qualitative effects. Tie to business or team goals. |
| `proof` | Screenshots of the real artifacts with links (see exports.md). |

## Voice

- Concrete nouns, active verbs: led, drove, shipped, unblocked, designed, scaled, rescued, simplified.
- Short sentences. No "leveraged", "spearheaded synergies", "passionate about", "in today's fast-paced world".
- Name collaborators generously; credit raises the subject (they lead people well).
- Angle notes from intake bias selection and ordering, and appear in the hero line.

## Painting the grass green (rules)

Allowed:
- Upgrading role verbs one step when evidence supports influence (contributor who drove decisions → "co-led").
- Rounding up (17.6% → "nearly 20%"), choosing the most flattering valid baseline.
- Stating plausible qualitative impact ("made weekly reporting self-serve for the team").
- Leaving out failures, or reframing them as learning ("first version flopped; the rebuild stuck").

Not allowed:
- Projects with no evidence.
- Invented numbers with units ("saved $2M") that no source mentions.
- Claiming work clearly owned by someone else.
- Fake quotes. Testimonials must be real quotes (trimmed is fine).

Tag every claim:
- `sourced`: directly stated in a source (include URL).
- `inferred`: reasonable reading of sources (include the URLs it is inferred from).
- `embellished`: framing beyond the sources (no fabricated facts; explain in `note`).

## Confidentiality

Default soften: revenue/ARR, customer names tied to deals, unreleased roadmap, headcount, security incidents. Replace with relative terms ("a top-10 enterprise customer", "double-digit growth"). Log each softening in `REVIEW.md`.

## `narrative.md`

The storytelling seed for the human: hero line options (3), a 150-word bio, each case study as prose (~200 words), a 60-second spoken pitch, and 5 bullet "brag points" ready for a perf review. Same claims, same tags inline as `[sourced]` etc.
